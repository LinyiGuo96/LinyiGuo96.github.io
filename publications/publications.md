### Constructing an Informative Prior Distribution of Noises in Seasonal Adjustment

M.Sc. thesis, University of Ottawa, 2020 · Supervised by Prof. Aaron Smith

Studies how state-space (hidden Markov) models can perform seasonal adjustment with results that match or beat conventional ARIMA-based methods.

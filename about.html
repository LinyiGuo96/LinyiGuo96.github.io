About · 关于

Hi, I'm
Lincoln.

I'm a software engineer in Scotiabank's Global Banking & Markets, building data pipelines and backend systems for equity derivatives and fixed income trading. I trained as a statistician, started in counterparty credit risk, and moved into engineering — so I care about both the numbers and the systems that produce them.

嘿，我叫郭林怡。目前在丰业银行全球银行与市场部（GBM）做软件工程师，为股票衍生品和固定收益交易搭建数据管道与后端系统。统计学硕士出身，从交易对手信用风险的量化分析起步，后来转向软件工程。
